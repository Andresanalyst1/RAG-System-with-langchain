## Publications & Academic Work

- **Contributor / Co‑author** on academic work during industrial engineering studies (related to educational technology / gamification).
Andres contributed to the academic article “Students’ preference for the use of gamification in virtual learning environments”, published in the Australasian Journal of Educational Technology. The research explored how gamification can improve motivation, engagement, and learning outcomes in online education. The study analyzed students’ preferences when using a gamified virtual learning tool called Didactic City, applying a quantitative methodology and structural equation modeling to evaluate factors such as usefulness, enjoyment, knowledge acquisition, ease of use, and motivation.

The article concluded that gamification can significantly enhance the learning experience in virtual environments by increasing student interest and reducing dropout rates, highlighting the importance of interactive digital strategies in modern education. Andres participated as a contributing author during his university studies, collaborating in the research process, data analysis, and academic writing.
